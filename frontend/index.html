How AI Viva Examiner Works

An end-to-end academic assessment architecture designed to ensure fair, rigorous, and repeatable examinations.

01

Curriculum & Scope Definition

Define the candidate's subject, academic tier, syllabus scope, and examination strictness parameters.

  • Configurable difficulty levels
  • Targeted topic domains
  • Expected competency matrix
02

Interactive Oral Assessment

The AI examiner delivers structured questions, listens to candidate reasoning, and presents dynamic follow-ups.

  • Follow-up probing questions
  • Misconception verification
  • Real-time response capture
03

Multi-Dimensional Evaluation

Every response is scored against academic rubrics including conceptual depth, accuracy, and communication clarity.

  • Clarity & vocabulary rating
  • Depth vs. superficiality test
  • Evidence-based scoring
04

Diagnostic Gap Analysis

A detailed diagnostic audit pinpointing exact topics requiring reinforcement with recommended study resources.

  • Conceptual blindspot map
  • Audit-ready session report
  • Structured review action items
